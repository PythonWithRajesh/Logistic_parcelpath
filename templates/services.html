Here are common answers about booking pickups, delivery timeframes, tracking, and enterprise freight.

Once you submit a booking through our Book Parcel form, our system automatically assigns a pickup executive closest to your location. The executive arrives within your chosen 2-hour window, scans the digital label, seals the package, and immediately generates a live tracking ID.

Rates depend on the actual weight or volumetric weight (whichever is higher), distance between origin and destination pincodes, and the selected delivery speed (Standard, Express Air, or Same-Day). You can use our interactive rate estimator above to preview costs instantly before booking.

Yes! Every standard parcel includes complimentary basic coverage up to ₹5,000. For high-value electronics, luxury items, or delicate freight, we offer comprehensive transit insurance covering full invoice value up to ₹5,00,000 with hassle-free claim settlement.
